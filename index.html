Interaction

Where and Who — drag to lasso individuals; in Where, alt-drag instead to filter by region. When — drag across to filter by age; one dot per individual, with present-day individuals in the left gutter and anything older than 12,000 BP in the right one. Everywhere: double-click and hold to pan, scroll to zoom, and right-click to clear the selection.

Layout

Drag the dividers to resize panels, drag a panel’s ⠿ grip onto another to swap them, or double-click any divider to restore the default layout.

Local sample overlay

CSV/TSV/Eigenvec stays in this tab. Required: geneticId, id, sample or IID. Optional: population, dateBP, dateSD, lat, lon, pc1, pc2, basis, snpsHit, haplogroups, publication and DOI.
